SHELTERIQ — APP DEMO VOICEOVER SCRIPT
3 minutes 00 seconds
======================================

[0:00–0:10] — OPENING (HOME SCREEN)
"Tonight, nearly 2,700 people in South Florida don't have a safe place to sleep.
ShelterIQ is an AI-powered Progressive Web App that helps people experiencing
homelessness find shelter, food, and services — instantly, on any phone."

[0:10–0:22] — HOME PAGE
"The app opens to a live stat dashboard: available emergency beds, updated in real time.
Users tap 'I need help finding services' — or outreach workers enter their own dedicated mode."

[0:22–0:42] — INTAKE FORM
"The intake form takes seconds. Choose what you need — shelter, food, mental health support,
veteran or youth services. Who you're with. And your area.
Hit Find Services — results appear instantly, no loading screen."

[0:42–1:00] — RESULTS PANEL (scrolling cards)
"Results are ranked by distance and walk-in availability.
Every card shows the address, hours, phone number, whether you can walk in without calling —
and a live community availability badge.
Green means 'usually has space.' Red means 'often full' — based on real check-ins."

[1:00–1:13] — CHECK-IN FLOW
"When someone is heading to a shelter, they tap 'I'm heading here.'
After arriving, they tap Yes or No — and that data anonymously updates
the availability badge for every future user. Crowdsourced shelter availability, in real time."

[1:13–1:25] — AI CALL SCRIPT
"Before calling, users get a personalized call script from Google Gemini AI —
exactly what to say, in plain language. Their situation, what the shelter accepts.
Available in English, Spanish, and Haitian Creole."

[1:25–1:40] — CRISIS DETECTION
"ShelterIQ detects crisis signals in real time.
Type a phrase indicating suicidal ideation or domestic violence —
the app interrupts immediately and surfaces 988, the National DV Hotline,
and the local crisis line. One tap to call. No extra steps."

[1:40–2:00] — MAP VIEW (markers + Safe Waiting Spots)
"The map opens directly to your location — no searching required.
It shows all 32 services with real geocoded coordinates.
Tap any marker for hours, a direct call button, and a Navigate option.
Toggle the Safe Waiting Spots layer to see public libraries,
county cooling centers, 24-hour locations, and transit hubs —
places to stay safe indoors while waiting for a shelter to open."

[2:00–2:22] — IN-APP NAVIGATION
"Tap Navigate on any result and the app enters navigation mode.
Choose your travel mode — Walking, Biking, Driving, or Transit.
The route draws on the map, green line the whole way.
Tap More to expand step-by-step directions — the map slides up
so you can see the route while you read the steps.
Tap Follow to re-center the map on your moving position.
Or tap Open App to hand off to Google Maps or Apple Maps
for full turn-by-turn audio — with the correct travel mode pre-selected."

[2:22–2:38] — OUTREACH MODE + INTELLIGENCE DASHBOARD
"Outreach workers get their own mode — natural language search across the full database.
Ask for 'veteran male substance abuse no ID' and get matched results instantly.
After multiple searches, the AI generates a gap analysis: which needs are most common,
which searches had no good matches. A real briefing tool for supervisors and case managers."

[2:38–2:52] — MULTILINGUAL + PWA
"Everything is translated — English, Spanish, and Haitian Creole.
The app is a Progressive Web App: installs on any phone from the browser,
works with cached results, and never requires an account or sign-in."

[2:52–3:00] — CLOSING
"ShelterIQ — built for the 2026 Congressional App Challenge, FL-25.
Real resources. Real people. Real time."
