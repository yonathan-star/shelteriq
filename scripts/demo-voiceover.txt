SHELTERIQ — APP DEMO VOICEOVER SCRIPT
2 minutes 30 seconds
======================================

[0:00–0:10] — OPENING (HOME SCREEN)
"Tonight in Broward County, nearly 2,700 people don't have a safe place to sleep.
ShelterIQ is an AI-powered web app — available as a Progressive Web App on any phone —
that helps people experiencing homelessness find shelter, food, and services instantly."

[0:10–0:22] — HOME PAGE
"The app opens to a real-time stat dashboard showing available emergency beds.
Users can tap 'I need help finding services' or — if they're an outreach worker — enter
their own dedicated mode."

[0:22–0:40] — INTAKE FORM (selecting shelter, alone, central Broward)
"The intake form takes seconds. Select what you need — shelter, food, mental health support,
veteran services, or youth services. Then who you're with, and what part of Broward County.
Hit Find Services — and the results appear instantly, no AI wait time needed."

[0:40–0:58] — RESULTS PANEL (scrolling cards)
"Results are ranked by distance and walk-in availability.
Every card shows the address, hours, phone number, whether you can walk in without calling —
and a live community availability badge. Green means 'usually has space.'
Red means 'often full' — based on real check-ins from other users."

[0:58–1:12] — CHECK-IN FLOW (tapping 'I'm heading here' → 'Yes, I got in')
"When someone is heading to a shelter, they tap 'I'm heading here.'
After arriving, they tap Yes or No — and that data anonymously updates
the availability badge for every future user. Crowdsourcing shelter availability
in real time."

[1:12–1:25] — AI CALL SCRIPT (tapping 'How to call')
"Before calling, users can get a personalized call script generated by Google Gemini AI.
It tells them exactly what to say — their situation, what the shelter accepts — in plain language.
Available in English, Spanish, and Haitian Creole."

[1:25–1:40] — CRISIS DETECTION (typing crisis keywords → banner)
"ShelterIQ also detects crisis signals in real time.
If someone types a phrase indicating suicidal ideation or domestic violence,
the app immediately interrupts — before any shelter search —
and surfaces the 988 Suicide Lifeline, the National DV Hotline, and Broward's local crisis line.
One tap to call. No extra steps."

[1:40–1:55] — MAP VIEW (with Safe Waiting Spots toggle)
"The map shows all 32 Broward services with real geocoded coordinates.
Clicking any marker shows hours, directions, and a direct call button.
Toggle on the Safe Waiting Spots layer to see public libraries,
county cooling centers, 24-hour locations, and transit hubs —
places to stay safe indoors while waiting for a shelter to open."

[1:55–2:10] — OUTREACH MODE / INTELLIGENCE DASHBOARD
"Outreach workers get their own mode — natural language search across the full database.
After multiple searches, the AI generates a gap analysis:
which needs are most common, which searches had no good matches.
A real-time briefing tool for supervisors and case managers."

[2:10–2:25] — MULTILINGUAL + PWA
"Everything is translated — English, Spanish, and Haitian Creole —
reflecting Broward's three largest unhoused populations.
The app is a Progressive Web App: it installs on any phone from the browser,
works offline with cached results, and never requires an account or sign-in."

[2:25–2:30] — CLOSING
"ShelterIQ — built for the 2026 Congressional App Challenge, FL-25.
Real resources. Real people. Real time."
